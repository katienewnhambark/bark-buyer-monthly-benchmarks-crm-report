What stands out

SMS earns £303 of PPR per 1,000 sends; marketing email earns £0.94

A 322× gap per message. SMS is 0.4% of buyer send volume and delivers more attributed project revenue per thousand than any other channel. Push Marketing is second at £153.

Volume sits almost entirely where return per message is lowest: 732M marketing emails against 3.1M SMS. That is the shape of the year, not a one-month anomaly.

SMS bounce rate is 5.18% — above the 5% amber line

Driven by January (77,620 bounces on 957,484 sends, 8.1%) and buyer_bob SMS at 8.59% across the year. Worth a number-quality pass on that group before it drags the channel average further.
